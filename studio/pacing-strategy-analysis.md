Here is a comparative analysis of your anchor plan alongside three divergent alternative pacing strategies, followed by an evaluation debate and final selection.

### Overview of Options

### Anchor: Complexity-Tapered Pacing (The Baseline)

-   **Structure:** Tapering daily template output as complexity increases.

    -   *Tiers 1--2:* 3 templates/day (\~53 days)
    -   *Tier 3:* 2 templates/day (40 days)
    -   *Tiers 4--5:* 1 template/day (200 days)

-   **Total Timeline:** **\~293 days (\~9.5--10 months)**

-   **Daily Time Commitment:** **\~60--90 minutes/day** (stablized across all tiers).

### Alternative 1: Sprint-and-Sabbatical (Burst-and-Consolidate)

-   **Structure:** Intensive 2-week active sprints completing templates at high volume, followed by a 1-week \"Consolidation Sabbatical\" dedicated exclusively to review, schema refactoring, and AI Studio system prompt integration.

    -   *Active Sprint (14 days):* 4 templates/day in Tiers 1--3; 2 templates/day in Tiers 4--5.
    -   *Sabbatical (7 days):* 0 new templates; focus on synthesis and real-world project builds.

-   **Total Timeline:** **\~210 days (\~7 months)**

-   **Daily Time Commitment:** **120--180 minutes/day** during sprint weeks; **0 minutes/day** during sabbaticals.

### Alternative 2: Fixed-Time-Box Daily Routine (Strict 60-Minute Governor)

-   **Structure:** A non-negotiable **60-minute daily timer**. Template quantity is entirely variable and secondary to time spent.

    -   *Tiers 1--2:* \~2--3 templates completed per session.
    -   *Tier 3:* \~1--2 templates completed per session.
    -   *Tiers 4--5:* \~0.5--1 template completed per session (complex Tier 5 FIR/SMT templates span across 2 days).

-   **Total Timeline:** **\~330 days (\~11 months)**

-   **Daily Time Commitment:** **Exactly 60 minutes/day** from start to finish.

### Alternative 3: Isomorphic Batching & Competency Compression

-   **Structure:** Grouping templates by shared structural invariants rather than completing them sequentially. The student identifies isomorphic patterns across a module, configures a single master schema in AI Studio, and tests templates in batches.

    -   *Tiers 1--3:* Batching 4--5 isomorphic templates/day.
    -   *Tiers 4--5:* Batching 2--3 isomorphic templates/day.

-   **Total Timeline:** **\~130 days (\~4.3 months)**

-   **Daily Time Commitment:** **\~90--120 minutes/day**.

### Debate Analysis

1.  **Alt 3 (Isomorphic Batching)** is enticing because it finishes the course in under 4.5 months. However, in Tiers 4 and 5---which involve complex formal intermediate representations, SMT solvers, and state compression---batching risks skipping the critical edge-case debugging that builds genuine prompt-architecture mastery.
2.  **Alt 1 (Sprint-and-Sabbatical)** provides excellent consolidation periods, but a 3-hour daily commitment during sprint weeks creates high friction and increases the likelihood of abandoned sprints due to real-life scheduling conflicts.
3.  **Alt 2 (Fixed-Time-Box)** is the ultimate habit builder and guarantees zero burnout. However, stretching the timeline to 11 months risks momentum decay in the final tiers.
4.  **The Anchor Plan** strikes the optimal equilibrium: it keeps daily commitment bounded (\~1--1.5 hours) while dynamically adjusting output expectations to match the rising cognitive density of higher tiers.

### Reflection & Winner Selection

### Winner: Modified Anchor Plan (Anchor + Sabbatical Checkpoints)

The **Anchor Plan (Complexity-Tapered Pacing)** is the winner, but with one key refinement borrowed from Alternative 1: **inserting a 3-day \"Integration Sabbatical\" at the completion of each Tier**.

### Reasoning for Selection

1.  **Solves the Core Constraint:** The foundational axiom of this curriculum is that *\"syntax decays, logic compounds\"*. Deep structural logic cannot be rushed. The Anchor\'s tapered pace (3/day 2/day 1/day) protects against cognitive flooding during the transition from basic attentional gating up to symbolic solvers and Markovian state compression.
2.  **Predictable Daily Habit:** Keeping daily study bounded to **60--90 minutes** avoids schedule disruption while maintaining steady forward momentum.
3.  **Sabbatical Buffer:** Adding a 3-day pause between Tiers adds only \~15 days to the total schedule (bringing total completion time to **\~10 months**), but provides dedicated space to build custom system prompts in Google AI Studio without accumulating cognitive debt.
